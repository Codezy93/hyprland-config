Visual Direction

From cyberpunk contrast to Tahoe softness

The theme now uses cooler blue-gray glass layers, muted white highlights, softer shadows, and broader rounded corners. Instead of neon-heavy accents, the setup leans on translucency, depth, and gentle cyan glow for focus states.

Waybar

Menu Bar feel

Styled as a floating translucent top bar rather than a hard-edged status strip.

Wofi

Spotlight feel

A centered search surface with smooth glass background and softer focus treatment.

Eww Dock

mac-like app bar

The old clock widget is replaced with a bottom-centered dock that launches existing apps and scripts already present in the setup.

No extras

Same stack, new feel

No plugins or additional packages were introduced. The theme shift comes from reworking Hyprland, Waybar, Wofi, Dunst, Kitty, Hyprlock, and Eww styling only.

Consistency

One visual language

The menu bar, notifications, launcher, lockscreen, and dock now all share the same Tahoe Glass palette and material treatment.
